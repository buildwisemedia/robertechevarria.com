The Talk

Running AI in a Real Business

Most talks on artificial intelligence (AI) cover chatbot prompts and content tricks. I show something different. I show what it looks like to run a business on AI agents, every single day.

I walk the room through where AI stands right now: chatbots first, then agents, then full autonomy. The next 18 months matter most for owner-led businesses.

Then I get specific. I share real examples from my own company. I also share examples from the service businesses we build for. No numbers. No client names.

I take live questions. I answer them straight.

Format: 20–30 minute talk plus open Q&A. Also works as a fireside chat or panel.
First delivered: June 25, 2026 at The Buckhead Club, Atlanta, in the Catalyst Collective "AI Conversations" series.
