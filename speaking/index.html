The core talk

Running AI in a real business.

Most AI talks start with the future. This one starts with the operating week.

Robert opens the curtain on what changes when AI moves from an experiment to part of the company: how work gets delegated, where trust breaks, why operator judgment matters more rather than less, and which decisions cannot wait for the technology to feel settled.

The room leaves with a practical model for separating useful capability from noise, plus a sharper answer to the only question that matters: what should we actually do with this now?

01

See where AI can carry operational work now, and where a human still owns the call.

02

Move from scattered experiments to a system that compounds what a small team can do.

03

Handle trust when AI touches customers, employees, and the decisions around them.

04

Leave with the decisions an owner-operator should make this quarter.

About Robert

An operator with the receipts.

Robert Echevarria runs an AI-native marketing agency for owner-led service businesses across North America.

His perspective comes from building the systems, changing how teams work, and living with the consequences when AI moves from a demo into the company. He speaks for leaders who want a rational view of the technology without the hype cycle or the fear theater.
