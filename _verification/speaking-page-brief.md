# PROJ-SPEAK-001 Phase 2 — Direction-Anchored Brief

## Job

Give an event organizer enough real proof, substance, and booking clarity to decide whether Robert Echevarria belongs in the room. This is a speaking page, not a generic personal-brand bio and not a Buildwise Media sales page.

## Audience

Event organizers, program chairs, peer-group hosts, and leaders assembling useful rooms for owner-led service businesses.

## Three anchors

1. **Operator proof:** Robert spoke at the Buckhead Club on June 25, 2026 about running AI inside a real business. The page should feel lived, current, and operational.
2. **Room proof:** Use real event photography, real speaking clips, and a concise reel. Let the room and the material prove the claim before adjectives do.
3. **Booking clarity:** Name the audience, core talk, concrete takeaways, adaptable formats, and a direct organizer-to-Robert booking path.

## Anti-trope

No generic keynote clichés. No stock or generated audience imagery. No fabricated testimonials, audience counts, applause claims, client results, or performance metrics. No Buildwise Media visual identity. No vague “transform your business” promise. No wall of logos, no inflated speaker superlatives, and no generic “Get Started” CTA.

## Primary offer

**Running AI in a Real Business** — a practical operator's talk for owners and leadership teams deciding what to deploy, where humans still matter, and what waiting another year actually costs.

## CTA

Ask organizers to **Bring this talk to your room** and email Robert directly with the date, audience, and format.

## State boundary

Authoring and staging are authorized. Production remains blocked until Robert reviews the rendered desktop and mobile preview and gives explicit visual approval.
